Input · MaskedTime — real label · focus ring · error

Every field renders a real <label htmlFor> (kills placeholder-as-label). 16px value = no iOS zoom. One focus-visible ring replaces the ~14 hand-rolled :focus{border-color} variants. MaskedTimeInput is the net-new #35 primitive: auto-inserts the colon, inputMode=numeric, mono value — it becomes the one mm:ss field the confirm forks + public timer/schedule all adopt.

Card — surface, nesting, spacing

Card = --stone + --border + --radius-md, padded on the scale. Nested content steps to --stone2/--divider. Section titles reuse the label style. Replaces the .sc-card/.ex-card/modal shells that each pick their own radius (6–16px) and padding.
