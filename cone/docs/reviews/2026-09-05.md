## Verdict

**Three blockers and two silent data-loss bugs.**

- **B1 + B2 — half the theme picker is broken.** `Quadro ao Vivo` is *illegible* on both light themes
  (`--cream` on a hardcoded `#111` measures **1.00:1** in spirit-blossom-light), and
  `--theme-accent`'s inline `#00b8d4` override drops six consumers to ~2:1 there, including the login
  page's only focus indicator. Both are one click from a coach's normal state. Neither was in the record.
- **B3 — every Estações block publishes as an empty card.** The whole Publicador export family
  reads `bl.exercises` directly instead of `blockExercises()`, which exists to flatten `stations`
  for exactly that type. Zero station handling anywhere in the family. It rasterises into the PNG
  the coach hands out — in the feature four sessions were just spent rebuilding.
- **H4/H5 — a submitted score can vanish with no error.** A stale `skipped: true` survives a re-log
  from 4 of the 5 logging writers, and every reader then correctly drops the entry. Root cause is a
  second, drifted declaration of the athlete-key list.
- **H7 — `createdAt` never reaches two public pages.** `mapResultRow` is forked four times; the two
  public forks omit the provenance column `0007` was added to recover.

Everything the record *claimed* measured true or better: RLS matches the docs exactly, the allowlist
is deny-all behind a correctly-hardened `SECURITY DEFINER` function, CI blocks on four gates (one
more than documented), the font strategy ships no unused weight, and **nine of ten architectural
invariants hold** — several verified far more thoroughly than the claim required.

Phase 1's predicted through-line half held. **TvController and Timer — the two surfaces the C0–C5
program never reached — do carry the worst of it**: B1, the worst a11y ratios, 37 of the 54 dead CSS
classes, the largest untested hook, and the only remaining `confirm()`. But B3 lands squarely inside
the *most recently* redesigned surface, so "unreviewed surfaces are the risk" is not the whole
story — a fresh pass can ship a gap just as easily, and B3 is the kind only real data finds
(Estações is rare enough that no one had exported one).

  sending an expired user JWT instead of falling back to the anon key is the bug; swallowing the

  logged in — i.e. the coach, on the pages he checks most.
