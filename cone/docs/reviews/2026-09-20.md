## The estimate — what clearing this costs

**Board after this pass: 67 open rows** (P1 8 · P2 28 · P3 16 · P4 15), up from 51.

**Pace basis — recent, as chosen** (Aug 1 – Sep 20): 16 active days across 51 calendar days =
**2.2 active days/week**; 10 plans shipped over September's 7 active days = **1.4 plans/active day**.

**Why row counts mislead here.** Raw throughput over that window was 77 items closed ≈ 10.5/week,
which would clear 67 rows in six weeks. That number is inflated by batch rows (plans/68, /79, /84,
/90 each closed 5). Converting rows to plan-units by size (XS ≈ ¼ plan batched · S ≈ 0.4 ·
S–M ≈ 0.5 · M ≈ 1 · M–L ≈ 1.5 · L ≈ 2.5) gives **52.8 units** for today's 67 rows.

| | rows | plan-units | active days | calendar |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Everything** | 67 | 52.8 | ~38 | **~17 weeks ≈ 3.9 months** (mid-Jan 2027) |
| **Cheapest-first 55%** | 37 | 12.9 | ~9 | **~4 weeks** (late Oct 2026) |
| The 45% left after it | 30 | 39.9 | ~29 | ~13 weeks |

🔑 **The 50–60% milestone is cheap and the rest is not.** 55% of the rows is **24% of the effort**.
The board is XS/S-heavy right now (34 of 67), so the milestone genuinely arrives in about a month —
but it leaves **76% of the work** in the remaining 30 rows, which is where every L and all 11 Opus
rows live, **including #82, #102, #30 and #31**. Clearing by volume does not move login closer; on
these figures #82 → #30 → #31 is ~7.5 units ≈ 5–6 active days ≈ **2.5 weeks of its own**, and it
sits entirely inside that tail. (Recorded per the user's decision to target volume, not the login
path — the dependency is labelled, not re-litigated.)

⚠️ **The board is not frozen, and that dominates the long estimate.** Icebox went 17 (Jul 1) → 36
(Aug 1) → 37 (Sep 1) → 51 (Sep 20) → **67** after this pass, while 152 items were closed overall.
Even in review-free stretches, plan close-outs file 1–3 rows each, so filings ≈ closings. On the
remaining mix throughput is ~3.9 rows/week against an observed filing rate of ~2–4/week. **At
current behaviour the long tail converges slowly or not at all** — the lever is the filing rate or a
deliberate cull, not speed. This pass was run "add findings only" by explicit decision, so no row
was proposed for closure.
